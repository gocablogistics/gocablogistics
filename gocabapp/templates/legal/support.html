GoCab Support

GoCab is a bike courier and delivery app connecting senders with couriers in Nigeria.

Need help?

Email us at support@gocablogistics.com and we'll get back to you. Include your account phone number and, if it's about a specific trip, the date and pickup/drop-off.

Common questions

I need to delete my account. You can do this from within the app under Profile → Delete account, or email us and we'll do it for you.

A trip went wrong. Report it from the trip in the app, or email us with the trip details.

I'm a driver and have a payout question. Email us with your registered phone number and we'll look into it.
